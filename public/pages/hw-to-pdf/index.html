    Convert handwritten notes to PDF

    Handwritten to PDF turns photos of notebook pages and assignments into clean, consistently formatted A4 PDFs that are easy to read and print. It is useful for students who want tidy copies of class notes, or anyone who needs handwritten pages in a shareable, printable format.

    1. Open the tool and sign in with a free Puter account (it powers the AI that reads your handwriting).
    2. Add clear photos of your handwritten pages. Drag them to reorder, or use the arrows.
    3. Start the conversion and wait while the pages are read and laid out.
    4. Review the result, then download a print-ready A4 PDF.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is Handwritten to PDF free?

    Yes, the tool is free to use. AI processing runs through your own free Puter account, so you sign in once.

    What kind of photos work best?

    Well-lit, straight-on photos with dark ink on plain paper give the most accurate result.

    Can I edit the PDF afterwards?

    Yes. Use the Edit PDF Text tool on this site to change text in the finished PDF.
