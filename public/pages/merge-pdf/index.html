    Merge PDF files in three steps

    Merge PDF combines several PDF files into one document. Everything runs in your browser, so your files are never uploaded to a server, which keeps private documents private.

    1. Choose two or more PDF files, or drag and drop them into the box.
    2. Drag the files into the order you want them to appear.
    3. Click merge and download the combined PDF.

    Frequently asked questions

    Are my PDF files uploaded anywhere?

    No. Merging happens on your device in the browser.

    Can I change the order of the files?

    Yes. Drag files to reorder them, or use the up and down arrows.

    Is there a limit on the number of files?

    There is no fixed limit in the tool; very large files depend on your device's memory.
