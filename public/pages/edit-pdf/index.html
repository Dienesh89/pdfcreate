Edit text in a PDF

Edit PDF Text lets you change the words in a PDF directly in your browser. It works especially well on PDFs created with this site's tools, which keep their text editable.

  1. Open your PDF in the editor.
  2. Click the text you want to change and type your edit.
  3. Download the updated PDF.

Can I edit any PDF?

It works best on PDFs made with this site. Scanned or image-only PDFs have no text layer to edit.
