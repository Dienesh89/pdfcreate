Choose the right PDF editing tool

The PDF Editor is a set of free online tools for fixing a PDF in your browser: edit text, rotate pages, zoom and resize. Choose the tool you need, upload your PDF and download the updated file.

  1. Pick Edit PDF Text to change words inside a PDF.
  2. Pick the page editor to rotate, zoom or resize pages.
  3. Open your PDF, make your changes and download the result.
