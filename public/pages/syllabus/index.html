    Create a syllabus PDF from a photo

    Syllabus Maker converts photos of a handwritten syllabus into a neat, consistently formatted syllabus PDF. The header appears once on the first page and the subjects follow in a clean table-style layout that is ready to print or share.

    1. Open the tool and sign in with a free Puter account.
    2. Upload photos of your handwritten syllabus.
    3. Start the conversion; the header and subject rows are read and arranged on A4 pages.
    4. Check the preview and download the print-ready syllabus PDF.
