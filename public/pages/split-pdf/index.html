Split PDF

Split a PDF by page ranges, every N pages, or pick the pages you want. Files stay on your device.

PDF

How do you want to split it?

Each comma-separated range becomes its own PDF. Example: 1-3, 5, 7-9 creates 3 files.

Use 1 to save every page as a separate PDF.

Tap pages to choose them. The chosen pages are saved together as one PDF.

Split a PDF in three steps

Split PDF breaks one PDF into smaller files by page range, every N pages, or the specific pages you select. It runs in your browser, so your file is never uploaded.

  1. Choose a PDF file, or drag and drop it into the box.
  2. Pick a mode: page ranges, every N pages, or selected pages.
  3. Click split and download the result. Several output files come as a ZIP.

Frequently asked questions

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No. Splitting happens on your device in the browser.

Can I extract just a few pages?

Yes. Use the selected pages or page range option.

What happens if I create several files?

They are bundled into a single ZIP file for download.
